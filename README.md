# Apple Inc. – Financial & Operational Performance Analysis (FY2024–FY2026)

Excel project analyzing Apple's sales, gross margin and regional performance
using the company's public annual report (Form 10-K).

## Objective
Evaluate how Apple's business evolved over three fiscal years and answer:
which products and regions generate the most revenue, which are the most
profitable, and what decisions management should consider.

## Data source
Apple Inc. Form 10-K for fiscal year 2026 (Item 7 and Item 8), filed on
2026-11-03, via SEC EDGAR. All figures in USD millions.

## Tools and skills
Excel Tables, SUMIFS, calculated columns, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts,
data validation checks, business analysis.

## What I did
1. Collected and structured data on net sales by category, region and gross
   margin by segment (Products vs Services).
2. Cleaned the data and verified totals across tables.
3. Built financial indicators: gross profit, gross margin %, operating margin %
   and year-over-year growth.
4. Created pivot tables and a dashboard.
5. Wrote conclusions and five business recommendations.

## Key findings
- Net sales grew from $391.0B (FY2024) to $478.8B (FY2026), and gross margin
  improved from 46.2% to 48.8%.
- Services is 25.8% of sales but 40.2% of gross profit (76.2% margin vs 39.3%
  for Products).
- iPhone is 53.1% of net sales: revenue is highly concentrated.
- Greater China grew 28% in FY2026 but has the lowest gross margin (46.1%).

## Limitations
Apple does not report gross profit by product category, so profitability can
only be compared between Products and Services and between regions. Quarterly
analysis is not included in this version.
